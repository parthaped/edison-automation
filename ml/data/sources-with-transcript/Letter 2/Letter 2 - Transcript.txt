Received Jan 5-1892 
Ans’d Jun 11th 1892 
Amsterdam, N.Y. Jan 4 1892 
[TAE Marginalia] I don’t think much of Vermont gold mines you might send me a sample + slate some particular I might send my man to see it. 
Dear Sir 
I have a very valuable mine in Vermont and it contains gold and silver that is my business prospecting for mineral and I write to know if you would like to invest in such a mine and I have found about ten more but have no title as yet only of the above mentioned. Please let me hear from you at your earliest convenience and oblige yours respectfully 
John Newell 
#83 Grove St Amsterdam N.Y.
